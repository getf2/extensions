# Security

Found an extension that does more than it says? One that reaches a host it
didn't list, sends a token somewhere other than its service, or takes
what it wasn't asked for. Or a way past the checks (`tools/check.mjs`), or
into the signed list without being merged here. Please tell privately
first, not in an issue:

- **Report a vulnerability**, under this repo's Security tab (GitHub keeps
  it private between you and the maintainers), or
- **Help › Send Feedback** in F2, which goes to its maker alone.

Say which extension and version, or what gets past what, and how to make
it happen. Every report gets read. An extension that's doing harm comes
out of the store first, and is fixed after.

A way out of the sandbox extensions run in is F2's own:
[getf2/f2](https://github.com/getf2/f2/security/policy).
